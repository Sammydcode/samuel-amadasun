About me

Machine learning engineering built on a data analytics foundation.

Profile

I am a machine learning engineer and data analyst with a BSc in Computer Science from the University of Benin (2021/2025 session, completed November 2025). I started in data analysis, and my interest in what models can do with data pulled me into data science, machine learning and deep learning. I built my practical ML foundation at Babtech Computers and have been building projects since.

Machine learning engineering

I work across the full model lifecycle: cleaning and preprocessing data, exploratory analysis, feature engineering, training and evaluating models with Python, Pandas, NumPy and Scikit-learn, and deploying them as usable applications with FastAPI and Streamlit.

My face recognition system shows this end to end. The model runs as a pipeline behind a FastAPI service, so a user uploads two faces and gets back whether they match, with a similarity score. I have also built classification and regression models (phishing detection, loan prediction, house price prediction) and an anomaly detector for credit card data.

Deep learning

I have built deep neural networks from scratch to understand how they learn, rather than only calling library functions. I completed Andrew Ng’s deep learning course and earned the certification, and I continue to build and optimize ML systems.

Data analysis

I began as a data analyst and still work as one: cleaning messy data, exploring it, and turning it into visuals and reports with SQL, Excel, Google Sheets, Power BI, Matplotlib and Seaborn. My analyses include a fellowship academic dataset and a company share investment study.

Teaching and mentoring

From September 2024 to February 2025, during my industrial training at Babtech Computers, I taught and mentored over 50 learners aged 10 to 50. I taught Microsoft Office, CorelDRAW, advanced Excel for data analytics, Python, Power BI and SQL. It keeps me practiced at explaining technical work in plain language.
